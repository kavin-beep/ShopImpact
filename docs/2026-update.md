# ShopImpact 2026 refresh

Reviewed 5 October 2026. Methodology: `useeio-2024-inr-reference-v3`.

## Updated data

The app uses the final USEEIO Supply Chain GHG Emission Factors v1.4.0 by Wesley Ingwersen and Ben Young, published by the Cornerstone Sustainability Data Initiative on 14 October 2025. It succeeds EPA v1.3. The CO2e workbook sheet uses IPCC AR6 GWP-100 and kg CO2e per 2024 USD, purchaser price. The published total includes margins; rounded components need not sum exactly to that total.

`reference kg CO2e per INR = published factor / 83.669281580941`

`estimated kg CO2e = INR price × reference category factor`

The World Bank 2024 annual-average rate matches the dollar price basis. The latest annual observation checked was 2025 (87.1584483507393 INR/USD), displayed for context only. A full-year 2026 observation was not available. This is a checked snapshot, not a live feed or measured 2026 product emissions. Current prices are not inflation-adjusted; results remain broad US-sector proxies, not India-specific lifecycle measurements.

Original selected rows, source units, dates, authors, URLs and the workbook SHA-256 are in data/emission_sources.json. INR factors retain twelve decimal places. Prices and results use Decimal half-up rounding. New/reused category pairs retain equal factors; no unsupported reuse discount is applied. Free goods produce zero in the spend model, not zero physical emissions. Badge rules are unchanged.

## Existing records

Saved records keep their stored estimates and methodology. New and edited entries use v3. A notice identifies histories with earlier estimates. How it works offers a checkbox and button to explicitly confirm recalculation. IDs, dates, brands, prices and goals are preserved; estimates, totals and badges may change. Download a backup first.

Earlier source/catalog snapshots are archived in data/archive/. Earlier EPA v2 and illustrative v1 backups require the older-backup opt-in, then estimates are recomputed. Current-format backups containing older-methodology rows also require this opt-in. Uploaded estimates are never trusted. The older 15-record fixture remains for migration tests.

## Design and verification

The refreshed header, source-review label, rounded navigation, metric cards and data-freshness panel improve clarity. Decorative leaf motion respects reduced-motion settings. All compulsory features remain available.

82 automated tests passed on 5 October 2026, including explicit backup migration, preserving original records, and confirmed UI recalculation. Tests use disposable databases; no real accounts were modified. Explanation PDFs and earlier screenshots describe the previous release and were not regenerated by this update. Real user feedback and full accessibility checks remain pending.
